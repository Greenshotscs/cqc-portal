💡 How this works for CQC
When a CQC inspector arrives, they will ask to see evidence across all quality statements. You can show them:
• LogMyCare for care plans, risk assessments and outcomes
• QCS for policies and procedures
• Flo for staff records, DBS and training compliance
• This portal for the overarching evidence log, incidents, feedback, mock inspection scores and your overall CQC rating

Use the “Log Evidence” buttons above to record summary evidence from each system into your evidence log, so everything is tracked in one place.

Regulation Tracker
Evidence mapped to CQC Regulations — how inspectors actually work
⚠️ CQC inspectors work from Regulations, not just quality statements. This tracker shows your evidence mapped directly to each Regulation so you can see exactly where you are strong and where you have gaps.
Audit Log
Internal audits and quality checks — the most common inspection failure area
🔍 Inspectors look for evidence that you audit yourselves regularly and act on findings. Audits that identify no issues are a red flag — good audits find things and show what you did about them.
Staff Supervision Log
Regular supervisions and appraisals — a Well-led requirement inspectors always check
👤 Every CQC inspector asks for supervision records. Staff should receive formal supervision at least every 8 weeks and an annual appraisal. Log all supervisions here.
🔒 DBS certificate numbers are recorded on the Right to Work / DBS form held in each staff member's individual folder on the ZA Drive. Direct inspectors to the ZA Drive for certificate verification.
Medicines Management
Medication safety, audits and error tracking — highest risk area for CQC rating
💊 Medication failures are the most common route to an Inadequate rating. Log all medication audits, errors, near misses and learning here. Inspectors will ask for this immediately.
Service User Involvement
Evidence that people are involved in their care — person-centred care in action
💜 Inspectors look for evidence that people are actively involved in planning and reviewing their own care — not just receiving it. Record care plan reviews, consultations and preferences captured here.
